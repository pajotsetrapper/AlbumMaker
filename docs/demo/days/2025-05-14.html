Bridges & the old town

Wednesday, May 14, 2025
Luzern
2025-05-15 →
← → keys or swipe change day · Esc returns to all days

We started early, before the tour groups, with the sun coming up over the lake and the old town still half asleep across the water.

From the Lion Monument we climbed onto the Musegg Wall for the view over the rooftops, crossed the river on the Spreuer Bridge and took far too long over lunch on the Kornmarkt.

In the afternoon the light turned warm and orange along the Reuss. We finished on the Chapel Bridge, of course.
